Country silhouettes derived from world-countries (https://github.com/mledoze/countries), whose GeoJSON outlines originate from thematicmapping.org/world_borders. Adapted database: outlines.json; license: ODbL.txt. North-up local azimuthal equal-area projection, individually scaled to fit. Disconnected regions are preserved. Generalized, historical boundaries may differ from the globe and modern borders; no statement of sovereignty is implied.
